Frequently asked

Everything indie founders want to know before installing a Stripe app.

Will this conflict with Stripe's built-in retries?

No. We replace Stripe's default retry schedule with our own (you toggle this on install) and disable Stripe's generic dunning emails so customers don't get duplicates. Both can be reverted in one click if you ever uninstall.

What does the AI actually do in the save flow?

Given the customer's tenure, plan, and stated cancel reason, the model picks one of your pre-approved offers (pause, discount, downgrade, feature-unlock) and writes a one-paragraph message in your tone. You hard-cap discount % and pause length in admin so the model can't bleed your margin.

Are we just paying you 5% of revenue we'd recover anyway?

No. The 5% is calculated on incremental recovery vs. Stripe's default behavior. Most teams see a 30-40 point lift on involuntary churn alone, and another 15-25% on cancel-flow saves. If we're not net-positive, we're free.

Do you support Paddle and Lemon Squeezy?

Stripe is GA. Paddle and Lemon Squeezy are on the public roadmap and shipping in Q2 2026. Email us if you want to be a design partner.

How long does install take?

Under five minutes for the free tier. Branded dunning takes another fifteen to set up your domain in Resend. The cancel-flow widget is one line of JS in your customer portal.
