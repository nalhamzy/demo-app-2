Common questions

Things SEO and content leads ask before they sign up.

How is this different from Profound or Otterly?

Profound starts at $500/mo and targets enterprise. Otterly is closer in price but lacks competitor diff and content briefs. MentionIQ ships a vertical buyer-intent prompt library, AI-generated briefs, and white-label for agencies — all from $99.

Do you really query the LLMs every day?

Yes. We make actual API calls to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Perplexity, in fresh sessions, with retry and rate-limit handling. We cache aggressively to keep costs predictable, but the answers you see are not synthetic.

What is the free audit, exactly?

Submit your domain. We auto-derive 25 buyer-intent prompts, run them against four LLMs, and email a PDF showing your share of voice, top three competitors, and the prompts where you're invisible. One-time, no card, no obligation.

Can the LLMs really change what they say about my brand?

Yes — mostly through what they retrieve at inference (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search) and what they trained on most recently. The content briefs target both: pages worth ranking organically and structured data the retrieval layer prefers.

Will my data be used to train models?

No. We use API tiers with training opt-out for every provider. Your prompts and tracked brands stay in your workspace.

I run an agency. Is white-label real?

Yes. The Agency plan gives you a custom subdomain, your logo, your color palette, and per-client logins. Bills come from us; reports look like yours.
