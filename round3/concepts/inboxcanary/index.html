Questions

Things people ask us in the r/coldemail DMs.

How is this different from MailReach or GlockApps?

MailReach and GlockApps are point-in-time test tools — you run a check, you see a score. InboxCanary is continuous: every domain, every hour, with alerting and a full historical reputation graph. We also ship an agency white-label and Smartlead / Instantly OAuth that those tools don't.

Do I need to change my DNS or MX records?

No. We read your existing SPF, DKIM, DMARC, BIMI, and MX records. We don't proxy your sending traffic.

What happens when an alert fires?

You get a Slack ping (or PagerDuty page on Growth) within sixty seconds, with a one-page summary: which domain, which signal, the suggested remediation, and the historical context for the alert. Most P2 alerts include a one-click "snooze if intentional" option.

Can I monitor a client's domain on their behalf?

Yes — that's the Agency plan. You get multi-tenant dashboards, per-client logins, and a white-label status URL like deliverability.youragency.com.

Where do the seed inboxes come from?

We maintain a network of 200+ live consumer accounts across Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo, refreshed continuously. Growth plans get cohort-segmented placement (e.g. SMB Gmail vs. enterprise Workspace) so you can see who's flagging you.

What if my domain gets flagged in the middle of the night?

You get paged on the alert channel of your choice. We also keep a public per-domain status page so your reps can self-serve check whether the silence is them or the system.
