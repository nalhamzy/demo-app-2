# MentionIQ - LLM Rank Tracker

## One-line pitch
Ahrefs for ChatGPT and Perplexity: tracks how often your brand is mentioned, recommended, or cited across LLM answers - and tells you what content to ship to fix it.

## Target user
SEO-aware marketing leads at $1M-$50M ARR B2B SaaS companies who watched 30% of organic search traffic shift to LLM answers in 2025-2026 and need to prove ROI on "AEO" (Answer Engine Optimization).

## Core feature (v1)
Daily query of 200-1,000 buyer-intent prompts ("best CRM for solar installers" etc.) across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity. Tracks brand mentions, citation domains, sentiment. Diffs vs competitors. Auto-generates content briefs targeting prompts where you don't show up.

## Distribution wedge
r/SEO (700k), r/bigseo, the Marketing OS Slack, and Twitter/X SEO community (#SEO). Sponsor 3 SEO newsletters (Marketing Brew, Detailed, Growth.Design) at $1.5k each. Free brand audit lead magnet.

## Pricing model
$99/mo starter (50 prompts), $299/mo growth (250 prompts), $799/mo agency (1,000 prompts + white label).

## Path to $10k MRR in 60 days
35 customers at avg $290 = $10,150. Existing competitors (Profound, Otterly, Peec) charge $500-2,000/mo - room for indie undercut. SEO newsletters convert ~1% on free-audit lead magnets to paid in 30 days.

## Build effort
14 days. LLM API loop + Postgres + dashboard.

## Tech stack suggestion
Next.js + Postgres + LLM API fanout (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Perplexity) + ClickHouse for analytics + Stripe.

## Why now
Google's AI Overviews + ChatGPT Search + Perplexity flipped the SEO market in 2025. Every CMO is being asked "how do we rank in ChatGPT?" and most have no answer. Profound raised $20M Series A in late 2025 - validates category, but their pricing leaves the SMB tier wide open.

## Defensibility
Historical citation database (90 days of cross-LLM data is a real moat), proprietary prompt-mining (taken from r/SEO real questions), agency white-label channel partnerships, and per-vertical buyer-intent prompt libraries.
