# ClipMint for Podcasters

## One-line pitch
Opus Clip but for B2B podcasters: turns one episode into 30 LinkedIn-native posts, 10 vertical clips, 3 newsletter drafts, and a YouTube chapter list - all auto-scheduled.

## Target user
B2B podcasters with 1k-50k downloads/episode (sales, marketing, founders) who already use Riverside or Descript and pay for Opus Clip but hate posting clips manually.

## Core feature (v1)
Drop in an episode link/file. Pipeline: transcribe -> identify 10 high-retention 60-90s moments -> generate vertical video with captions + brand template -> draft platform-native LinkedIn/X copy per clip -> schedule via Buffer/Typefully API. Brand voice training on past 20 posts.

## Distribution wedge
r/podcasting (250k), r/podcasts, the Podcast Movement Slack, and direct outreach to the top 500 B2B podcasts on Listen Notes. Sponsor 3 podcasters' newsletters at $500 each for guaranteed 5k impressions to peers.

## Pricing model
$49/mo for 4 episodes/mo, $129/mo for 12, $299/mo for unlimited + brand voice. Annual 2 months free.

## Path to $10k MRR in 60 days
85 customers at avg $120 = $10,200. Listen Notes top-500 outreach = 500 cold emails, 15% reply, 30% trial, 50% paid = ~11. Reddit + newsletter sponsorships drive remainder.

## Build effort
15-18 days. FFmpeg + Whisper + Claude + Remotion for branded video.

## Tech stack suggestion
Next.js + Remotion (programmatic video) + Whisper-3 + Claude Sonnet + Buffer/Typefully API + Stripe.

## Why now
Opus Clip raised $20M at $215M valuation (2024), proving demand but they target consumer creators. B2B podcasters need brand voice + LinkedIn-first formatting Opus doesn't do. Remotion 4.0 (2025) made templated video rendering trivially cheap.

## Defensibility
Brand-voice fine-tunes per customer (high switching cost), LinkedIn algo expertise (post format, character limits, hook patterns), and a curated B2B clip-quality model trained on top-performing posts.
