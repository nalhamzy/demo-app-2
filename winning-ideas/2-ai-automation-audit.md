## Sharpened concept

**Working name: AuditFlow** — a flat-fee $499 "AI Automation Audit" for service-based SMBs (agencies, law firms, real-estate teams, accounting practices) with 5-50 employees. The wedge: instead of selling abstract "automation consulting," sell a concrete deliverable — a recorded 60-min Loom walkthrough of their stack with a written 5-automation roadmap and ROI estimate per workflow. The $499 audit converts ~40% into a $2,500 build engagement and ~20% into a $1,500/mo retainer. Target customer: agency or professional-services owner doing $500k-$5M ARR who is drowning in repetitive ops.

## Offer / pricing

- Automation Audit (foot-in-door): $499 flat, delivered in 5 business days
- Single Build engagement: $2,500 flat per automation
- Retainer (Growth): $1,500/mo, up to 4 build hours + maintenance
- Retainer (Scale): $3,500/mo, weekly office hours + unlimited small fixes

## 7-day launch plan

- Day 1: Pick a vertical (start with marketing agencies). Set up Make + n8n + OpenAI accounts.
- Day 2: Build 3 demo automations: lead-to-CRM, invoice-to-Sheets, content-repurpose pipeline.
- Day 3: Record 3 Loom walkthroughs of the demos. Write a one-page audit deliverable template.
- Day 4: Build a one-page site with the $499 audit offer, demo videos, Stripe Payment Link.
- Day 5: Post on LinkedIn with a public "I will audit the first 3 agencies free" hook. DM 30 agency owners.
- Day 6: Run free audits, collect testimonials, get the case-study screenshots.
- Day 7: Switch the offer to paid. DM 30 more. Aim for 1 paid audit booked by EOD.

## First 3 customers playbook

- LinkedIn: target marketing agency owners (use Sales Navigator filter: "Marketing Agency" + 5-50 employees). 20 personalized DMs/day, lead with a specific automation idea for their business.
- Indie Hackers / r/agency / r/smallbusiness: post a free teardown of an anonymized stack each week, soft-CTA to the audit.
- Cold email to local agency lists scraped via Apollo: 50/day, single-line opener + Loom of a relevant demo.

## Risks & mitigations

- Risk: clients want custom builds beyond audit scope. Mitigation: hard-cap the $499 deliverable at 5 days and 5 recommendations; everything else is a build SOW.
- Risk: AI/automation tools change pricing or break workflows. Mitigation: standardize on n8n self-hosted as a fallback; charge maintenance retainer to cover drift.
- Risk: undercut by overseas freelancers on Upwork. Mitigation: niche by vertical and lean on case studies + same-timezone responsiveness as the moat.
