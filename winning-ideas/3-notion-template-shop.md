## Sharpened concept

**Working name: StackKit** — a Notion template shop laser-focused on one ICP: solo agency operators (1-5 person marketing/design/dev agencies). The wedge: instead of generic "second brain" templates, ship a tightly opinionated "Agency OS" — client CRM, project tracker, proposal generator, retainer dashboard, and SOP library — that drops in on day one of a new agency. One flagship $79 bundle, three $29 single-purpose add-ons, and a $19 monthly "template club" with one new drop per month. Distribution: TikTok/YouTube Shorts walkthroughs plus the Notion Template Gallery.

## Offer / pricing

- Agency OS bundle (flagship): $79 one-time
- Single templates (CRM-only, Proposal Generator, Retainer Dashboard): $29 each
- Template Club subscription: $19/mo (new template + monthly office hours)
- Lifetime pass: $199 one-time (everything past + future)

## 7-day launch plan

- Day 1: Build the flagship Agency OS template in Notion (CRM + projects + proposals + SOPs).
- Day 2: Design 5 cover images in Canva. Write the sales copy with a clear before/after.
- Day 3: List on Gumroad with the $79 bundle and three $29 single-template SKUs.
- Day 4: Submit to the official Notion Template Gallery. List on Whop and Lemon Squeezy as backups.
- Day 5: Record a 60-second TikTok and a 90-second YouTube Short walking through the CRM view.
- Day 6: Post 3 short demos on X and LinkedIn tagged with #NotionTemplate and #AgencyLife.
- Day 7: DM 25 small-agency owners offering a free copy in exchange for a quote. Convert quotes into landing-page social proof.

## First 3 customers playbook

- TikTok / YouTube Shorts: 1 demo/day for two weeks targeting #AgencyLife and #FreelanceTips; bio link to Gumroad.
- Reddit: r/agency, r/freelance, r/Notion — answer questions with screenshots from the template, soft-link in profile.
- Notion creator Twitter/X circle: reply to top Notion creators' threads with template clips; DM after engagement.

## Risks & mitigations

- Risk: Notion changes a feature and breaks the template. Mitigation: ship a "v2025.1" versioning scheme and email buyers free updates.
- Risk: easily knocked off by other creators. Mitigation: depth of the bundle + branded SOP library + active Template Club community as the moat.
- Risk: cold start with no audience. Mitigation: 30-day Shorts sprint plus 5 free seeded copies to micro-influencers in the agency niche for testimonials.
