# Niche Coaching Sprint

## Why this won

| Criterion | Score |
|---|---|
| Speed to first dollar | 4 |
| Low startup cost | 5 |
| Income ceiling | 4 |
| Differentiation / defensibility | 4 |
| Solo-founder feasibility | 5 |
| **Total** | **22 / 25** |

Coaching wins because there is literally nothing to build before the first dollar lands: a Calendly link, a Stripe checkout, and a clear promise. Defensibility comes from the operator's lived expertise plus public proof, which gets stronger every week. It scales from $0 to $10k/mo using the same one-person stack.

## Sharpened concept

**Working name: Sprint90** — a productized, four-week 1:1 coaching package for one specific transformation in one specific role. The wedge: instead of generic "career coaching," pick a single before/after (e.g. "junior PMs landing a senior PM offer in 90 days," or "solo accountants signing their first 10 retainer clients"). One ICP, one outcome, one price. Buyers get four weekly calls, a private Loom feedback loop, and a Notion workbook. Sold off a one-page Carrd site with three testimonials and a paid intro-call CTA.

## Offer / pricing

- Paid intro / triage call: $97 (60 min, credited toward sprint)
- Sprint90 four-week package: $1,500 flat
- Continuation retainer after sprint: $750/mo (2 calls + async)
- Group cohort (once 5 sprints sold): $600/seat, 8 seats max

## 7-day launch plan

- Day 1: Pick the exact ICP and transformation. Write the one-line promise. Buy domain.
- Day 2: Build a Carrd one-pager with promise, bio, FAQ, Stripe Payment Link.
- Day 3: Set up Calendly with paid $97 intro call. Connect Stripe.
- Day 4: Write 3 LinkedIn posts and 3 X posts: case study (even if past unpaid), framework, contrarian take.
- Day 5: DM 25 ideal-fit prospects with a personal offer for a free 15-min triage in exchange for feedback.
- Day 6: Run the calls. Convert at least one into a paid intro at $97.
- Day 7: Run the paid intro. Pitch Sprint90 at the end. Close first sprint.

## First 3 customers playbook

- LinkedIn: search the exact ICP title (e.g. "Product Manager" + "Series A"). Send 20 personalized DMs/day referencing a specific post they wrote.
- X: reply substantively to 10 ICP posts/day for two weeks; pitch DM only after a real exchange.
- Niche Slack/Discord communities (e.g. Lenny's, Reforge, Indie Hackers, role-specific subreddits): answer 5 questions/day with depth, soft-link to free triage call in profile.

## Risks & mitigations

- Risk: niche is too broad and pitch lands flat. Mitigation: narrow the ICP further every week until DMs get >20% reply rates.
- Risk: time-for-money cap. Mitigation: convert the workbook + recordings into a self-serve cohort by month three.
- Risk: no testimonials initially. Mitigation: run 3 free pilot sprints week 1 in exchange for written + video testimonials.
