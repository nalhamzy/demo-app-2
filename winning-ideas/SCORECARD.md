## Key takeaways

- The two winners (Coaching, AI Automation Consulting) both productize an existing skill and require zero inventory. Service businesses with a clear deliverable beat marketplaces and ad-driven plays in 2026 for solo speed-to-revenue.
- Notion Template Shop wins the #3 slot over Virtual Assistant on tiebreak: every sale is leverage rather than a billable hour.
- Highest-ceiling solo idea (AI Micro-Tool, 19) is a strong long-game candidate but loses on speed. Worth revisiting once any of the top 3 is generating $5k/mo.
