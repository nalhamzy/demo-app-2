# Website Flipping on Flippa / Empire Flippers

## Summary
Buy underperforming content sites or small SaaS, fix them up over 3-6 months, and resell at 30-40x monthly profit. Flippa marketplace transaction value grew 36% in 2025; SaaS deals jumped 73.5%.

## Speed to first dollar
Long. First flip 3-9 months; flip cycles thereafter ~4-6 months.

## Startup cost
Medium. $1,000-$10,000 to acquire a starter site; budget $500-$2K for fixes (content, SEO, design).

## Skills needed
Site valuation (SDE multiples), basic SEO, content strategy, due diligence (traffic + revenue verification), light WordPress/Webflow.

## Income potential
Highly variable: $2,000-$30,000 per flip after 3-6 months of work; experienced flippers run 2-4 flips/year.

## How to start
- Study sold listings on Flippa, Empire Flippers, and Motion Invest to internalize multiples.
- Acquire one $2K-$5K content site with stable organic traffic and clear improvement levers.
- Execute 90-day improvement plan: refresh top 10 posts, fix internal links, add an email list, monetize with Mediavine/Ezoic + affiliates.
- Wait until the trailing-12-month revenue stabilizes higher, then list at 35-40x SDE.
- Reinvest proceeds into a slightly larger asset; compound into a portfolio over 18-24 months.

## Why this is different from anything in round 1
Round 1 had zero asset-acquisition ideas; this is buying, improving, and reselling income-producing internet assets - a capital-intensive M&A play, not a service or content creation hustle.
