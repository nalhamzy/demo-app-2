# Paid Newsletter on Substack or beehiiv

## Summary
Build a niche paid newsletter and earn from subscriptions plus direct sponsorships. Substack reached 8.4M paid subscriptions in Q1 2026 and ~100K publications now earn money on the platform.

## Speed to first dollar
2-6 weeks for first paying subscriber; same week for first sponsorship if list >1,000.

## Startup cost
Low. $0-$50/month (free tiers on Substack/beehiiv; optional custom domain ~$12/yr).

## Skills needed
Consistent writing, narrow point of view in a defined niche, basic audience growth (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Reddit, cross-recommendations).

## Income potential
$200-$5,000/month within 6-12 months for a focused niche; top operators clear $20K/month. Sponsorship CPMs run $10-$75 ($50-$100+ for B2B); flat sponsor placements typically $50-$250 for newer creators.

## How to start
- Pick a sharp niche (e.g., "ops tooling for dental clinics") and commit to a weekly cadence.
- Open free tier first; turn on paid ($5-$10/mo) once you have ~30 weekly active readers.
- Use beehiiv Boosts or Substack Recommendations to grow via cross-promotion from day one.
- Pitch 5 relevant SaaS sponsors per week once you cross 1,000 subscribers; price flat at $150-$300/placement.
- Track CTRs to justify rate increases each quarter.

## Why this is different from anything in round 1
Round 1's affiliate idea was about commission revenue from a niche site; this is a subscription + sponsorship publishing business with recurring MRR, not pay-per-click commissions.
