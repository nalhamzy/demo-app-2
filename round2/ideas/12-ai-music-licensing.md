# AI-Generated Music Licensing and Sync

## Summary
Generate original instrumental tracks with Suno or Udio and license them on YouTube/TikTok via DistroKid, plus direct sync to creators. Suno/Udio Pro plans grant commercial rights; you keep 100% of streaming royalties.

## Speed to first dollar
3-6 weeks (DistroKid release window + first stream royalties); direct sync to creators can pay within days.

## Startup cost
Low. ~$30-$50 (Suno Pro $10/mo, DistroKid $23/yr, optional $20 cover art).

## Skills needed
Musical taste, prompt engineering for audio, basic mastering (LANDR/CloudBounce), metadata and ISRC hygiene.

## Income potential
$50-$1,000/month from streaming pennies; $500-$5,000/month if you bundle into themed packs sold on a Gumroad/Pond5/AudioJungle store. Note: ASCAP/BMI do not currently collect on AI-generated music, so streaming + sync direct is the path.

## How to start
- Subscribe to Suno Pro or Udio paid plan (commercial rights require active paid status at generation).
- Generate 50 tracks in one niche (lo-fi study, chillhop, cinematic trailer beds, podcast intros).
- Master, tag with metadata, and release in EPs of 5-8 tracks via DistroKid.
- Upload non-exclusive licenses to Pond5, AudioJungle, and a self-hosted Gumroad pack ($15-$30).
- Pitch 20 YouTubers/podcasters per week with custom theme music ($150-$500 per commission).

## Why this is different from anything in round 1
Round 1 had no audio/music products - this monetizes a generative-audio output as both a streaming asset and a sync-licensed product, with platform-specific legal nuances (commercial rights, PRO collection gaps) round 1 never touched.
