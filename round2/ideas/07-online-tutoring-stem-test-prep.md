# Online Tutoring (STEM, Test Prep, Coding)

## Summary
Tutor online via Wyzant, Wiingy, Tutor.com, or direct. STEM and programming tutors earn $40-$100/hour in 2026; weekly payouts on platforms like Wiingy.

## Speed to first dollar
1-2 weeks once profile is approved; direct clients (parent FB groups) can book within days.

## Startup cost
Low. $0-$50 (webcam, whiteboard tool like Miro/Excalidraw, optional $20 background check).

## Skills needed
Subject mastery (calculus, Python, SAT/ACT, MCAT, etc.), patience, ability to explain step-by-step.

## Income potential
$15-$25/hour entry; $40-$100/hour specialized; $1,500-$5,000/month part-time at 10-15 hrs/week.

## How to start
- Pick the highest-paying subject you actually know (AP Calc, Python, MCAT bio, AMC math).
- Apply to Wyzant + Wiingy + Tutor.com simultaneously for fastest first booking.
- Record a 60-second intro video and 1 sample mini-lesson for your profile.
- Ask for a written review after every session; reviews compound bookings on Wyzant.
- After 10 reviews, raise rate and recruit students into a group cohort ($30/seat x 6 = $180/hr).

## Why this is different from anything in round 1
Round 1 had no education/tutoring category at all; this monetizes domain knowledge through synchronous teaching with platform-driven demand and weekly pay, distinct from coaching (which targeted founders/professionals, not students).
