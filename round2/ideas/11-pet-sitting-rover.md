# Pet Sitting and Dog Walking on Rover

## Summary
Become a Rover sitter offering walking, drop-in visits, daycare, and overnight boarding. Rover keeps ~20%; sitters typically net $15-$40/walk and $25-$75/overnight.

## Speed to first dollar
1-2 weeks (background check + first booking).

## Startup cost
Low. $35 background check; optional $50-$150 for crates, leashes, GPS tracker.

## Skills needed
Comfort with dogs/cats, reliability, clear written communication, basic scheduling.

## Income potential
$300-$800/month part-time; sitters who add boarding regularly clear $1,500-$5,000/month.

## How to start
- Sign up at rover.com/become-a-sitter and complete the background check.
- Add 8-10 photos with multiple dog breeds; write a specific "about me" (other-pet experience matters).
- Start with drop-in visits and 30-min walks to build 5-star reviews fast.
- After 10 reviews, add overnight boarding (highest $/hour service).
- Move repeat clients off-platform later only if your ToS allows; keep new acquisition on Rover.

## Why this is different from anything in round 1
Round 1's gig work was rideshare/delivery (driving people and packages); this is in-person pet care - a different platform ecosystem, different physical skill, and recurring relationships rather than one-off rides.
