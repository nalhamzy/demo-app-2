# Etsy Digital Downloads (Curated, Not Generic)

## Summary
Sell digital products on Etsy: interactive PDFs, Goodnotes stickers, ChatGPT prompt packs, niche SVGs, wallpapers. Bestsellers cross 200K sales; the market in 2026 rewards curated systems over AI slop.

## Speed to first dollar
1-4 weeks; Etsy's existing buyer traffic produces sales without external marketing.

## Startup cost
Low. $20-$50 (listing fees $0.20 each, Canva Pro $13/mo, optional $30 mockup pack).

## Skills needed
Design taste, niche research, SEO copywriting for Etsy search, basic Canva/Figma/Procreate.

## Income potential
$300-$3,000/month within 6 months for a focused 30-50 listing shop; top sellers run six figures.

## How to start
- Pick a buyer (e.g., "ADHD adults," "homeschool moms," "bullet-journal beginners") and one format.
- Use eRank or Alura to find low-competition keyword pockets with $-buying intent.
- Ship 20 listings in 30 days; use buyer-specific mockups, not generic flat lays.
- Offer fillable interactive PDFs (Acrobat) - a 2026 differentiator buyers explicitly want.
- Reinvest first $200 in promoted listings for top performers only.

## Why this is different from anything in round 1
Round 1's Notion templates targeted productivity buyers on Notion's niche marketplace; Etsy digital downloads sell to a much broader consumer audience (planners, stickers, art, SVGs) with completely different SEO mechanics and product formats.
