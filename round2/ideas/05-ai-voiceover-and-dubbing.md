# AI-Assisted Voiceover and Video Dubbing

## Summary
Sell voiceover and multilingual dubbing services using tools like Murf, ElevenLabs, and HeyGen. Single projects pay $50-$500; localization retainers exist for creators going global.

## Speed to first dollar
3-10 days. Voiceover gigs on Voices.com, Fiverr, and Upwork close fast for new sellers with strong demos.

## Startup cost
Low. $30-$60/month (ElevenLabs Creator + Murf Pro + a $50 USB mic for hybrid human/AI work).

## Skills needed
Audio editing basics, ear for delivery/pacing, light scriptwriting, knowledge of licensing and voice-clone consent rules.

## Income potential
$500-$4,000/month part-time; specialists in YouTube channel localization (one creator = many languages) can recur $2K-$10K/month per client.

## How to start
- Pick one wedge: explainer videos, audiobook narration, or YouTube channel dubbing into 3 languages.
- Build 5 sample reels (different tones) and host on a one-page portfolio.
- List on Voices.com, Fiverr, and Twine; pitch 20 YouTubers/week running unlocalized channels.
- Price per finished minute ($30-$80/min) instead of per word for predictable margins.
- Use ElevenLabs Dub for first pass, then human-edit pacing for premium tier.

## Why this is different from anything in round 1
Round 1's faceless YouTube idea used AI voices to publish your own channel; this sells voiceover/dubbing as a service to other creators and brands, with licensing/consent and reel-based sales mechanics.
