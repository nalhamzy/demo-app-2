# Paid Research Studies and Focus Groups

## Summary
Get paid as a research participant on Respondent.io, User Interviews, and Fieldwork. Online focus groups in 2026 typically pay $50-$250/hour, with specialist studies (medical, tech execs) up to $400+/session.

## Speed to first dollar
1-3 weeks from sign-up to first study; payouts typically arrive within days of completion via virtual Visa or PayPal.

## Startup cost
Low. $0. Webcam, quiet room, and a strong demographic profile.

## Skills needed
Articulate verbal communication, honesty during screeners, fast response to study invites.

## Income potential
$200-$1,500/month realistic; participants in tech/finance/medical can hit $2,000+ in busy months.

## How to start
- Create profiles on Respondent.io, User Interviews, Fieldwork, FocusGroups.org, and Prolific.
- Fill profile fields completely (job title, tools used, conditions); honesty wins recurring invites.
- Enable email + push notifications; studies fill in minutes.
- Target moderated 60-90 min sessions ($75-$200) over short surveys ($5-$20) for best $/hour.
- Keep one device for screeners; a second for actual sessions to avoid tech issues.

## Why this is different from anything in round 1
Round 1's "website and app testing" was unmoderated 15-min UX tests at $10-$60; this is moderated qualitative research and focus groups paying 5-10x more per session, with completely different platforms (Respondent vs UserTesting).
