# Remote Bookkeeping for Small Businesses

## Summary
Offer monthly bookkeeping retainers to small businesses using QuickBooks Online, Xero, or Wave. No CPA license required in the US/Canada; experienced bookkeepers earn $40-$80/hour.

## Speed to first dollar
2-4 weeks to first paying client through warm network; longer for cold outbound.

## Startup cost
Low. $50-$200/month (QBO ProAdvisor free, Wave free, optional Bookkeepers.com training $150-$2K).

## Skills needed
Double-entry accounting basics, attention to detail, QuickBooks/Xero proficiency, monthly reconciliation discipline.

## Income potential
$900-$1,100/month for a side hustler at 11-16 hrs/week; $4,000-$8,000/month full-time with 8-12 retainer clients.

## How to start
- Become a free QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor; finish their certification in a weekend.
- Productize three packages: $300/mo (basic), $600/mo (with payroll), $1,000/mo (with reporting).
- Get first 2 clients via personal network and local FB business groups.
- Use Karbon or Keeper for client portal; automate close checklist to scale to 8+ clients.
- Network with CPAs and tax preparers - they refer overflow bookkeeping work all year.

## Why this is different from anything in round 1
Round 1's virtual assistant idea was generalist task work (email, scheduling, research); this is a regulated-knowledge service with monthly recurring retainers, specific software certifications, and a buyer (small business owner needing books closed) who pays significantly more.
