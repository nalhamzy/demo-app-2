## Themes Observed in 2026 Research

- Recurring revenue (subscriptions, retainers, MRR) consistently outperforms one-off transactions across categories.
- Specialization wins: vertical AI agents, niche newsletters, and STEM/test-prep tutors earn 3-10x generalists.
- Cross-platform leverage matters: pair generation tools (Suno, ElevenLabs, Sora) with distribution rails (DistroKid, TikTok Shop, Maven, Etsy) rather than picking one.
- Asset-acquisition plays (domain and website flipping) are re-emerging in 2026 as AI-native buyers reprice digital durability.
