# Sell a Cohort-Based Course on Maven

## Summary
Run a 2-4 week live course on Maven teaching a specific job-relevant skill. Maven instructors keep 90% of revenue (10% platform fee) and earn 1.5x more via Maven's marketplace distribution.

## Speed to first dollar
4-8 weeks: 2 weeks landing-page + waitlist, 2-4 weeks pre-sales, then run the cohort.

## Startup cost
Low. $0 upfront on Maven; ~$200 for Loom, Notion, and Zoom premium during cohort.

## Skills needed
Genuine expertise in the topic, course design (outcome-driven curriculum), live facilitation, strong landing-page copy.

## Income potential
$5,000-$50,000 per cohort (e.g., 25 seats x $1,000); 4 cohorts/year = mid-five to six figures.

## How to start
- Pick a transformation a busy professional will pay $500-$1,500 for (e.g., "Ship your first AI agent in 4 weeks").
- Build a Maven landing page and open a waitlist; aim for 100 sign-ups before pricing.
- Run a free 60-min webinar to convert waitlist; close 15-25% to paid seats.
- Deliver 4 live sessions + 1 office hour/week + a Slack community.
- Collect testimonials and outcomes; raise price 25-50% next cohort.

## Why this is different from anything in round 1
Round 1's coaching idea was 1:1 calls billed hourly; cohort-based courses are leveraged 1:many synchronous teaching with a fixed start date, peer cohort, and a productized outcome - 10-50x revenue per delivery hour.
