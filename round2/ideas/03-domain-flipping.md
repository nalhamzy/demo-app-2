# Domain Name Flipping

## Summary
Hand-register or buy expiring domains targeted at AI-startup, DeFi, and new-TLD niches and resell them on Sedo, Dan, or Afternic for 10x-1000x markup.

## Speed to first dollar
Variable. First flip typically 4-8 weeks; some flippers list and sell in days using Dan.com BIN pricing. Months 1-2 usually $0-$200.

## Startup cost
Low. $10-$15 per domain registration; $200-$500 starter portfolio of 15-30 domains. Optional Estibot appraisal $20/mo.

## Skills needed
Naming intuition, basic SEO/keyword research, negotiation, patience. No coding.

## Income potential
$300-$800/mo months 3-4; $800-$2,000/mo months 5-8; experienced flippers $1,000-$3,000/mo at 8-15 hrs/week.

## How to start
- Pick 1-2 themes with active buyer demand: AI tools, agent names, blockchain, vertical SaaS.
- Use ExpiredDomains.net + GoDaddy auctions; register only brandable .com or premium .ai under $50.
- List immediately on Dan.com and Afternic with BIN price 20-50x cost.
- Reach out cold to startups using close keyword variants of your domains.
- Reinvest 50% of profits into a larger 30-50 domain rotating portfolio.

## Why this is different from anything in round 1
Round 1's "sell used items" is physical liquidation; this is a digital-asset arbitrage business with no inventory, no shipping, and a multi-month hold cycle.
