## Sharpened concept

LoopCrate is a tightly curated catalog of royalty-free AI-generated music packs sold to podcasters and indie video creators. Instead of "1,000 generic AI tracks," LoopCrate ships 8-track themed packs aligned to specific show formats: "True-Crime Cold Open," "B2B SaaS Interview Bed," "Finance Daily Brief," "Wellness Sleep Story." Wedge: each pack includes the cold-open sting, mid-roll bumper, outro, and 3 full-length beds at matching tempo and key, so a podcast host can theme their entire show in one $39 purchase instead of stitching across 5 marketplaces.

## Offer / pricing

- Single pack: $39 (8 tracks, full sync rights, perpetual license).
- 3-pack bundle: $89 (any three packs).
- "All Access" annual: $149/yr - unlock every pack including monthly new drops.
- Custom theme commission: $300-$500 (one show, bespoke 6-track pack delivered in 5 days).

## 7-day launch plan

- Day 1: Subscribe to Suno Pro ($10) and Udio paid ($10). Register DistroKid ($23/yr). Buy domain loopcrate.co.
- Day 2: Generate the first 30 candidate tracks for Pack 1 ("True-Crime Cold Open"). Curate the 8 best.
- Day 3: Master via LANDR ($5/track) or CloudBounce. Tag ISRC, write metadata, package as zipped WAV + MP3.
- Day 4: Build a Gumroad storefront with 1 pack live + 3 "coming next week" placeholders. Add a 30-second preview reel for each.
- Day 5: Generate Pack 2 ("B2B SaaS Interview Bed") and master. Release Pack 1 also to Pond5 and AudioJungle as non-exclusive.
- Day 6: Pitch 30 indie podcasters via PodMatch + Listen Notes; offer free pack in exchange for "music by LoopCrate" credit in show notes.
- Day 7: Launch on Product Hunt + r/podcasting. Run a 48-hour 30%-off launch promo on the bundle.

## First 3 customers playbook

- Channel 1: r/podcasting and r/PodcastGuestExchange - free-pack giveaways gated on a credit + email signup.
- Channel 2: PodMatch and Podchaser DMs to shows under 5K downloads/episode (they cannot afford Audio Jungle subscriptions but want better-than-Garageband).
- Channel 3: Twitter/X "podcast-twitter" cluster - public thread teardown of how 5 popular shows mismatch their music to their tone, with LoopCrate alternatives suggested.

## Risks & mitigations

- AI-music legal ground shifts (PRO collection rules, source-data lawsuits): always generate on an active paid Suno/Udio account, retain receipts, and route only through DistroKid + direct sync (avoid PRO-dependent revenue).
- Marketplace commoditization: the "show-format pack" concept is the moat - matched-tempo bundles, not loose tracks.
- Streaming royalties are pennies: do not lean on Spotify revenue; the model is direct pack sales + custom commissions.
