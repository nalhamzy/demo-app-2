## Sharpened concept

DubDeck is a done-for-you YouTube localization service for English-language creators with 50K-500K subscribers. We take their back-catalog and ship dubbed, lip-synced versions in three languages (Spanish LATAM, Brazilian Portuguese, Hindi) onto secondary channels they own. Wedge: we are not "a Fiverr voiceover gig" and not "an enterprise dubbing studio" - we are a 5-videos-per-month retainer at a price point a solo creator can actually approve, with consent paperwork and pacing edits handled.

## Offer / pricing

- Pilot: 1 video, 3 languages, $349 flat (loss-leader to land the retainer).
- Starter retainer: 4 videos/month x 3 languages = $1,200/mo.
- Pro retainer: 8 videos/month x 3 languages + thumbnails localized = $2,400/mo.
- Add-on: extra language pack (French, German, Japanese) +$300/language/month.

## 7-day launch plan

- Day 1: Buy domain dubdeck.io. Set up ElevenLabs Creator + HeyGen Video Translate trials. One-page Carrd site with three pricing tiers and a Loom demo placeholder.
- Day 2: Pick one public-domain or CC-BY YouTube clip, produce a 90-second before/after demo in ES/PT/HI. Embed on the Carrd page.
- Day 3: Build a 50-channel target list (50K-500K subs, English-only, niches: finance, productivity, fitness). Use vidIQ + manual filter.
- Day 4: Send 25 personalized cold DMs/emails referencing one specific video and a free 60-second sample of it dubbed.
- Day 5: Send the next 25 outreach + post a public Twitter/X thread "I dubbed [Creator]'s video into Spanish in 14 minutes - here is what broke."
- Day 6: Take the first reply call; offer the $349 pilot with a 5-day delivery promise.
- Day 7: Ship the pilot. Ask for a testimonial-on-camera in exchange for a 25% retainer discount in month 1.

## First 3 customers playbook

- Channel 1: r/NewTubers and r/PartneredYoutube - post a free teardown of one creator's localization opportunity (estimated extra views per language).
- Channel 2: Cold email mid-tier creators sourced via Social Blade "rising" lists in the 50K-500K band; reference one specific video by title in the first line.
- Channel 3: Twitter/X reply-guy in the creator-economy space (Colin and Samir, Paddy Galloway threads) with public dub demos; DM anyone who likes the demo.

## Risks & mitigations

- AI dub quality varies wildly by source audio: only accept clean-audio source files; offer a 100% refund on the pilot if pacing fails.
- Voice-clone consent / platform ToS shifts: keep written consent on file for every voice cloned; default to ElevenLabs preset voices unless the creator explicitly licenses their own.
- Commoditization by YouTube's native auto-dub: defensibility is the human pacing pass + thumbnail and title localization, which auto-dub does not do.
