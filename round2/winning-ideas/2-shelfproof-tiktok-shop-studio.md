## Sharpened concept

ShelfProof is a one-person TikTok Shop studio that goes deep on a single vertical: clean-ingredient kitchen gadgets and pantry tools for home cooks. The host (you) becomes the recognizable face for "the gadget reviewer who actually cooks dinner with it." Wedge: 90% of TikTok Shop creators are pan-vertical promo machines; ShelfProof's audience trusts that every product on the live has been used in a real recipe that week, which compounds repeat buyers and unlocks brand-direct deals at 2-3x baseline commission.

## Offer / pricing

- Phase 1 (weeks 1-6): pure TikTok Shop affiliate commissions, 5-20% per sale, target $500/mo.
- Phase 2 (weeks 6-16): negotiate brand-direct rate cards, $300-$800 per dedicated live segment + commission override.
- Phase 3 (month 4+): "Featured on ShelfProof" sponsored slot, $1,500/live for 10-min product hero spot, max two slots per live.

## 7-day launch plan

- Day 1: Apply to TikTok Shop Affiliate (need 1K followers - if below, set up creator account and post day 1 content). Order ring light + phone tripod ($60).
- Day 2: Pick 5 inexpensive kitchen gadgets you already own. Film 5 "honest 30-second review while cooking" videos. Post 2.
- Day 3: Post the other 3 videos. Request samples for 10 more SKUs through TikTok Creator Marketplace.
- Day 4: Post 2 new shorts. Draft a 45-min live show rundown: 6 products, recipe-in-the-background format.
- Day 5: Run a 45-min trial live (even with low followers); save replay as a portfolio asset.
- Day 6: Post 2 more shorts using clips from the live (compounding content). DM 5 small kitchen-tool brands directly with the live replay.
- Day 7: Schedule next week's two lives at fixed times (Tue/Thu 7pm ET). Review which video earned the first attributable sale and double down on that format.

## First 3 customers playbook

- Channel 1: TikTok Shop Creator Marketplace - request samples from 20 brands per week in the kitchen-tools category; many will offer commission overrides for early creators.
- Channel 2: Cold-DM founder accounts of indie kitchen-gadget brands on Instagram and LinkedIn with your live replay link and a one-line CPA pitch.
- Channel 3: Reddit r/cooking and r/Cooking4Kids organic seeding - post recipe content (not affiliate links) that drives profile visits and TikTok follows.

## Risks & mitigations

- Algorithm cliff: post 2 shorts/day for 30 straight days to give the algorithm enough signal; do not rely on any single video.
- Account ban / ToS shift: keep all email subscribers from a "recipe of the week" newsletter as an off-platform asset.
- Burnout from on-camera cadence: batch-shoot 8 shorts in one Sunday session; outsource only editing (Fiverr at $5/clip) once revenue justifies.
