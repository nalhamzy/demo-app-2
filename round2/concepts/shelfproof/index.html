The method

Three phases. Six weeks each. No vanity metrics.

A staged launch designed for a solo founder with a $60 ring-light budget. Each phase has a single revenue lever and a single audience signal we're trying to earn.

i.
Weeks 1-6 / Phase one
Affiliate posture
Pure TikTok Shop affiliate commissions, 5-20% per sale. Two shorts a day for 30 straight days to feed the algorithm. Target $500/mo while we build the on-camera reps.
ii.
Weeks 6-16 / Phase two
Brand-direct rate cards
Negotiate $300-$800 per dedicated live segment plus commission override. Brands pay because they see receipts, not impression estimates.
iii.
Month 4 onward / Phase three

"Featured on ShelfProof" sponsored slot at $1,500 per live, 10-minute product hero spot. Hard cap of two slots per live. Scarcity is the price.
