How it works

From English source to three live channels in five days.

A repeatable five-step pipeline combining ElevenLabs Dub, HeyGen lip-sync, and a human pacing pass on every cut.

STEP 01
Source intake
You drop the master file. We accept clean-audio sources only and run a 5-minute QC before quoting.
STEP 02
AI dub pass
ElevenLabs Dub generates the base track in ES LATAM, BR PT, and HI. HeyGen handles lip-sync.
STEP 03
Human pacing edit
A native-speaker editor adjusts cadence, idiom, and silence beats. This is the part auto-dub never does.
STEP 04
Localize the wrapper
Translated thumbnail, title, description, and tags - so the algorithm actually finds the right audience.
STEP 05
Publish & track
We upload to your secondary channels on a schedule and report watch-time deltas at month end.
