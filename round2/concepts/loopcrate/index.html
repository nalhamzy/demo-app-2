Four steps. No PRO entanglement.

Every pack is generated on an active paid Suno + Udio plan, mastered, ISRC-tagged, and shipped as zipped WAV + MP3. The legal posture is the moat.

// STEP 01
Format intake
Pick a show format - true-crime cold-open, B2B SaaS interview, finance brief, sleep story. We design the pack around it.

Generate & curate
30 candidate tracks generated on Suno Pro + Udio paid. We curate the 8 best matched in tempo, key, and mood.

Master & tag
LANDR mastering at $5/track. ISRC tags assigned. Metadata written. Packaged as zipped WAV + MP3.

Ship & license
Available on the LoopCrate Gumroad with full sync rights and a perpetual license. No PRO collection, no surprises.
