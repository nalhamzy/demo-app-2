# Notion Template Shop

## Summary
Build and sell Notion templates (CRMs, content calendars, finance trackers, second brains). Among the fastest-moving digital products in 2026, with 80-95% profit margins.

## Speed to first dollar
3-10 days

## Startup cost
Low ($0 — Notion is free; optional $10/mo for Gumroad Pro or Whop)

## Skills needed
- Strong Notion fluency (databases, relations, formulas)
- Basic graphic design for cover images (Canva)
- Understanding of one niche audience's workflow

## Income potential
$200-$500/month per template within 6 months; bundles can reach $2,000-$8,000/month.

## How to start
- Pick a niche audience (freelancers, real-estate agents, students, indie hackers).
- Build one flagship template that solves a specific pain.
- List on Gumroad ($19-$39) and Notion's official Template Gallery.
- Post tutorial walkthroughs on TikTok/YouTube Shorts.
- Add a 3-5 template bundle to lift average order value.
