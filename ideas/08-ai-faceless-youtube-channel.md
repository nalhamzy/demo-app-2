# AI Faceless YouTube / Shorts Channel

## Summary
Use AI to script, voice, and edit short-form and long-form videos in niches like finance, history, tech news, or motivation — without ever appearing on camera. Monetize via AdSense, affiliates, and digital products.

## Speed to first dollar
3-8 weeks (YouTube monetization threshold) — affiliates can pay sooner

## Startup cost
Low-medium ($30-$100/mo for ElevenLabs + a script LLM + stock footage subscription)

## Skills needed
- Niche research and topic selection
- Prompt-writing for scripts
- Basic video editing (CapCut, Descript, or Pictory)

## Income potential
$0 in first month, ramping to $500-$10,000/month by month 6-12 with consistent uploads.

## How to start
- Pick a niche with high RPM (personal finance, AI tools, tech reviews).
- Set up a content pipeline: LLM script -> ElevenLabs voiceover -> Pictory/CapCut edit.
- Post daily Shorts and 1-2 long-form videos per week.
- Apply for YPP at 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days).
- Layer affiliate links and a digital product once monetized.
