# User-Generated Content (UGC) Creator

## Summary
Brands pay creators to film short-form videos demonstrating products — no large following required. UGC is one of the most underrated and fastest-growing income streams in 2026.

## Speed to first dollar
1-2 weeks (time to film a portfolio + land first brand)

## Startup cost
Low-medium ($50-$300 for a ring light, tripod, and possibly a few sample products)

## Skills needed
- Smartphone video filming
- Basic editing (CapCut, InShot)
- On-camera comfort and clear narration

## Income potential
$1,000-$8,000/month part-time. Single videos commonly pay $150-$500.

## How to start
- Film 3-5 spec UGC videos for products you already own to build a portfolio.
- Post the portfolio to a free site (Notion, Beacons, or a simple Carrd page).
- Pitch brands directly via Instagram DMs and apply on UGC marketplaces (Insense, Billo, JoinBrands).
- Charge per-video (start at $100-$200) and raise rates after 5 paid jobs.
