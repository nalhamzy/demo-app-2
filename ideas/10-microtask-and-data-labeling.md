# Microtask and AI Data Labeling

## Summary
Complete small tasks — image tagging, data labeling, audio transcription, search-result evaluation — on platforms like Remotasks, Outlier, Scale AI's contributor portal, Clickworker, and Appen. Daily withdrawals on most platforms.

## Speed to first dollar
1-3 days

## Startup cost
Low ($0 — only need a laptop and reliable internet)

## Skills needed
- Attention to detail
- Reading comprehension (English or other supported language)
- Specialty bonuses for coders, writers, and STEM grads (LLM RLHF tasks)

## Income potential
$200-$2,500/month. AI RLHF tasks for STEM/coding contributors pay $30-$50/hr.

## How to start
- Sign up on Outlier, Remotasks, Clickworker, and Appen simultaneously.
- Complete onboarding qualifications — these unlock higher-paying queues.
- For coders/writers: target LLM training projects which pay 3-5x base rates.
- Withdraw earnings daily/weekly via PayPal or direct deposit.
