# AI Workflow Automation Consulting

## Summary
Build small AI + Zapier/Make/n8n automations for SMBs (lead routing, invoice parsing, content repurposing). "Even simple automations are worth hundreds or thousands of dollars" to companies in 2026.

## Speed to first dollar
1-2 weeks

## Startup cost
Low ($20-$80/mo for Make/Zapier + an LLM API key)

## Skills needed
- Comfort with no-code tools (Make, Zapier, n8n)
- Basic API/JSON understanding
- Ability to map a business process into steps

## Income potential
$2,000-$15,000/month. Single automations bill $500-$3,000; retainers $1k-$5k/mo.

## How to start
- Build 2-3 demo automations on common pain points (lead-to-CRM, invoice-to-Sheets).
- Record loom walkthroughs and post them on LinkedIn targeting agency owners and SMBs.
- Offer a flat-fee "automation audit" ($250-$500) as a foot-in-the-door.
- Productize 3 packages (starter / growth / retainer).
- Hire a VA to handle discovery calls once you hit 5 retainers.
