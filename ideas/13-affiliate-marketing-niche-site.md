# Affiliate Marketing Niche Site or Newsletter

## Summary
Promote other companies' products via unique tracking links and earn commissions on sales. In 2026, the fastest-paying angle is a tight niche newsletter or short-form social account rather than a slow SEO blog.

## Speed to first dollar
1-4 weeks (newsletter/social) or 3-6 months (SEO blog)

## Startup cost
Low ($0-$100 — domain, email tool like Beehiiv/Kit free tier)

## Skills needed
- Picking a profitable, underserved niche
- Persuasive writing and honest product reviews
- Basic SEO or social distribution

## Income potential
$100-$500/month in the first 90 days; $2,000-$20,000/month at scale with a 5-10k engaged audience.

## How to start
- Pick a niche where buyers research heavily (software, finance, hobbies with gear).
- Join 3-5 affiliate networks (Amazon Associates, Impact, ShareASale, PartnerStack).
- Launch a free newsletter on Beehiiv with a clear weekly format.
- Publish honest comparisons and "best X for Y" round-ups.
- Track clicks per link and double down on what converts.
