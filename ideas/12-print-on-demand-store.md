# Print-on-Demand Store

## Summary
Sell original designs on shirts, mugs, posters, hoodies, and other products without holding inventory by routing orders through Printful, Printify, or Gelato. AI tools now handle most of the design work, making this a low-effort e-commerce entry point in 2026.

## Speed to first dollar
3-14 days (depends on traffic strategy; faster with paid ads or existing audience)

## Startup cost
Low ($0-$100 — Shopify/Etsy fees, optional ad spend)

## Skills needed
- Basic design taste (or ability to direct an AI image tool)
- Understanding of a niche audience and their humor/aesthetic
- Light copywriting for product titles and descriptions

## Income potential
$200-$3,000/month for a part-time store; top niche stores clear $10k+/month.

## How to start
- Pick one passionate, narrow niche (e.g., pickleball moms, mechanical keyboard fans).
- Generate 20-40 designs with Midjourney/DALL-E and refine in Canva.
- Connect Printful or Printify to a Shopify or Etsy storefront.
- Drive traffic via Pinterest, TikTok organic, or low-budget Meta ads.
- Double down on the 3-5 designs that sell; kill the rest.
