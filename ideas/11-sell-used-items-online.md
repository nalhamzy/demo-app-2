# Sell Used Items Online

## Summary
Liquidate clothes, electronics, books, and furniture you already own through eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Poshmark, Mercari, or Depop. Still one of the fastest ways to generate cash online in 2026.

## Speed to first dollar
Same day to 3 days (Facebook Marketplace local pickup is fastest)

## Startup cost
Zero (you already own the inventory)

## Skills needed
- Phone photography
- Honest item descriptions
- Basic shipping/packing

## Income potential
$200-$2,000 one-time from a typical household; $1,000-$5,000/month if you scale into reselling thrift finds.

## How to start
- Walk through your home and pull every item unused for 12+ months.
- Photograph each in good light against a plain background.
- List on Facebook Marketplace (local) + eBay (national) for highest velocity.
- Price 20% below comparable sold listings to move fast.
- Reinvest profits into thrift / estate-sale flipping if you want recurring income.
