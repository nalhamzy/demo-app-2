# AI Prompt Engineering Services

## Summary
Sell custom prompts and prompt-systems to businesses that already pay for AI tools but struggle to get usable output. In 2026, "people who know how to talk to AI properly" can monetize that skill faster than many traditional professionals.

## Speed to first dollar
1-3 days (faster if you sell on Fiverr/Gumroad)

## Startup cost
Low ($0-$50 — a ChatGPT/Claude subscription and a Gumroad/Fiverr account)

## Skills needed
- Strong written English
- Hands-on experience with at least one major LLM (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)
- Understanding of a niche (marketing copy, SEO, sales emails, code review, etc.)

## Income potential
$500-$5,000/month for a part-time operator; high-end prompt-engineers selling to B2B clients clear $10k+/month.

## How to start
- Pick one narrow niche where AI output is poor by default (e.g., LinkedIn posts for SaaS founders).
- Build a 20-50 prompt pack that solves that niche end-to-end.
- List on Gumroad ($19-$39) and offer a custom-prompt service on Fiverr ($25-$100).
- Post short before/after demos on X and LinkedIn to drive traffic.
- Upsell into monthly retainers for prompt maintenance.
