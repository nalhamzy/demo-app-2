# Fiverr Quick Gigs

## Summary
Package a single skill (logo design, voiceover, copy edit, video cut, etc.) into a fixed-price gig and start receiving orders within days. Fiverr's pre-packaged "gig" model is the fastest freelance starting point in 2026.

## Speed to first dollar
2-7 days from listing

## Startup cost
Low ($0-$50 for software like Canva Pro or CapCut Pro)

## Skills needed
- One marketable micro-skill done well
- Decent gig copy/thumbnail
- Quick communication

## Income potential
$200-$3,000/month for new sellers; $5,000+/month for top-rated sellers.

## How to start
- Pick one narrow service you can deliver in under 2 hours.
- Study the top 10 gigs in that category — copy their structure, not their work.
- List 3 packages ($15-$40 starter; $50-$100 standard; $150+ premium).
- Promote your gig link on Reddit, X, and LinkedIn for the first week to seed reviews.
