# Virtual Assistant Services

## Summary
Handle email, scheduling, research, light bookkeeping, or social media for busy founders and executives. Platforms like Fancy Hands pay daily, while direct clients pay weekly retainers in 2026.

## Speed to first dollar
1-7 days (faster on a-la-carte platforms)

## Startup cost
Low ($0-$50 — laptop, decent internet, optional LinkedIn Premium)

## Skills needed
- Strong written communication
- Calendar / inbox / CRM tool fluency (Google Workspace, Notion, HubSpot)
- Reliability and discretion

## Income potential
$10-$35/hour for general VA work; $40-$60/hour for specialized (bookkeeping, legal, exec). Full-time VAs commonly clear $3k-$6k/month.

## How to start
- Pick a specialty (real estate VA, podcast VA, e-commerce VA) so you can charge more.
- Sign up to Fancy Hands, Time etc, or Belay for instant inbound work.
- Build a one-page service site with packages (10/20/40 hours per month).
- Pitch 20 niche founders per week on LinkedIn or X DMs.
- Ask for testimonials after week 1 and raise rates every 3 clients.
