# Website and App Testing

## Summary
Companies pay $10-$60 per test for users to record themselves walking through their websites and apps and narrating the experience. Quick, beginner-friendly, and pays within days.

## Speed to first dollar
2-5 days (after platform approval test)

## Startup cost
Low ($0 — most platforms only require a laptop, mic, and webcam)

## Skills needed
- Clear spoken English (or other tested language)
- Ability to think aloud while clicking through interfaces
- Working webcam and microphone

## Income potential
$100-$1,500/month part-time; high test-volume users hit $2,000+.

## How to start
- Sign up on UserTesting, Userlytics, TryMyUI, PlaytestCloud, and Validately.
- Pass each platform's qualifier test (usually 5-10 minutes).
- Set up a quiet, well-lit recording space.
- Check dashboards 2-3 times/day; tests get claimed fast.
- Reinvest first earnings into a better mic to qualify for higher-pay studies.
