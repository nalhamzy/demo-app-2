# Coaching and Consulting

## Summary
Package existing expertise (a trade, a career skill, a hobby you mastered) into 1:1 coaching calls or short consulting engagements. One of the fastest ways to monetize because there's nothing to build — you sell what you already know.

## Speed to first dollar
3-7 days

## Startup cost
Low ($0-$100 for Calendly + Stripe + a simple landing page)

## Skills needed
- Verifiable expertise in one area
- Listening and structured advice-giving
- Basic sales / discovery-call skills

## Income potential
$1,000-$15,000/month part-time. Calls range $75-$500 each; retainers $1k-$5k/mo.

## How to start
- Define the exact transformation you deliver (e.g., "I help junior PMs land their first senior role in 90 days").
- Set up a Calendly + Stripe checkout for a paid intro call.
- Post 3 case studies / testimonials on LinkedIn and X.
- DM 20 ideal clients per day with a personal offer for the first 2 weeks.
- Productize the engagement into a 4-6 week package after 3 paid clients.
