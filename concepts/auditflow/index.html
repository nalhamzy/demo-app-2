FAQ

Honest answers before you wire $499.

Will the $499 audit actually fit my business if I'm not a marketing agency?

The audit format generalizes across agencies, law firms, real-estate teams, and accounting practices. The throughline is service-based SMBs with 5-50 employees doing $500k-$5M ARR. If your model is different (e-commerce, SaaS, manufacturing) we'll say so on the intake form before you pay.

What stops scope from creeping past the audit?

The deliverable is hard-capped at 5 days, 5 recommendations, and a one-page roadmap. Anything beyond that is a separate Build SOW at $2,500 per automation. You see that price before the audit ships, so there is no surprise quote.

What if Make or OpenAI changes pricing or breaks a workflow?

Every recommendation in the roadmap carries a vendor risk flag and a fallback plan. We default to n8n self-hosted as a vendor-independent option whenever possible. Retainer clients get drift maintenance baked in.

How is this different from hiring an Upwork freelancer for a few hundred dollars?

Two things: vertical depth and timezone responsiveness. The audit is shaped by case studies in your specific niche, and replies happen in business hours of your timezone. If lowest price is your only criterion, Upwork is fine - just expect to manage scope yourself.

Do I have to commit to a build after the audit?

No. About 40% of audits convert to a build, ~20% to a retainer, the rest take the roadmap and run it themselves. You own everything in the deliverable, full stop.
