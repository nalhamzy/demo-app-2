StackKit ships under a v2026.x versioning scheme. If Notion changes a feature, I patch the template and email all buyers a new duplicate link, free. Lifetime pass holders get every future major version included.

What stops other creators from cloning this?

The bundle's depth (six wired modules) plus the SOP library plus the active Template Club is the moat. Anyone can copy a CRM table; it's much harder to copy 20 versioned SOPs and a monthly drop cadence.

Is this for solo operators or full agencies?

Built for 1-5 person marketing, design, and dev agencies. Solo operators get the most leverage. Larger agencies (10+) outgrow some modules - we recommend the Lifetime pass and pairing with a dedicated PM tool.

Do I need to be a Notion power user?

No. If you can duplicate a page and edit a database, you're fine. The bundle ships with a 12-minute setup walkthrough plus a YouTube playlist for each module.
