FAQ

Common questions.

What if my niche is too broad and the sprint doesn't fit?

The intro call exists to filter exactly that. If your jump is more general than the sprint promise, we'll narrow it on the call or refer you out. The $97 is credited toward the sprint only if we proceed.

Do you guarantee an offer?

No coach honestly can. What I guarantee is the artifacts, the call cadence, and the Loom loop. Most sprinters who do the work have a written offer in hand within 90 days of starting.

What happens if I miss a week?

The Loom loop keeps moving even when calls slip. We reschedule the live call inside the same week or roll it into the next, no penalty.

I'm not a junior PM. Will this still work for me?

The sprint format generalizes, but I only run sprints for transformations I've personally lived. If you're outside that band, the intro call is the cheapest way to find out — and you'll leave with a plan either way.

How is this different from a generic career coach?

One ICP, one outcome, one price. Generic coaches sell a flexible container. Sprint90 sells a single before-and-after, which is why it works in four weeks instead of six months.

What about after the sprint ends?

You can keep the momentum on a $750/mo continuation retainer (two calls + async) or graduate. Most sprinters take a 30-day break and then come back for a second arc.
